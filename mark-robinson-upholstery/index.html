Project questions

Thinking about
reupholstery?

Example prompts to help visitors prepare a useful enquiry.

Can I ask about a specific piece of furniture?

Describe the item and its condition, and ask the upholsterer whether the project is within the services offered.

Can I choose my own fabric?

Fabric choices and sourcing options should be discussed directly with the business and confirmed before work begins.

How can I get an estimate?

Send a few clear photos, approximate dimensions and a description of the work you have in mind, then confirm the next steps with the upholsterer.
