What a finished site could include

More than a nice homepage.

The goal is to make the business easier to understand and contact, not just make a pretty page.

01 / Explain

Clear services

Dedicated sections explain the main services in everyday language so visitors quickly understand what is offered.

02 / Show

Real photographs

Owner-approved photos of finished work, premises, tools, materials or customer-approved examples build trust better than stock imagery.

03 / Connect

Useful contact routes

Phone, email, location and social links can be checked and made easy to use on mobile.

04 / Finish

Owner-approved details

Before launch, confirm exact prices, opening hours, qualifications, service descriptions and any booking arrangements.

Proposal status

Independent concepts, ready for feedback.

These previews are not official live sites. Images and example copy should be replaced or approved by each business before launch. If interested, the next step is to confirm real services, prices, photos, contact details and requested changes.
